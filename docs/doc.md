## How to Get Started in Minutes  
1. **Choose a platform** – sign up on the app or website.  
2. **Verify your identity** – upload driver’s license, photo ID, and a selfie.  
3. **Meet vehicle requirements** – age, model year, and condition guidelines (usually a 4‑door car, 5‑passenger capacity, no major cosmetic damage).  
4. **Upload insurance proof** – personal auto insurance that meets the platform’s minimum liability coverage (often $100k/$300k).  
5. **Complete background check** – most platforms run an automated check within 24‑48 hours.  
6. **Set up payment** – link a bank account or debit card for direct deposit.  
7. **Activate quick‑start bonuses** – many platforms offer a sign‑up bonus after completing a set number of trips or deliveries within the first week.  

## Maximizing Earnings  
- **Peak‑hour strategies** – work during lunch (11 am‑2 pm) and dinner (5 pm‑9 pm) rushes for food delivery; evenings and weekends for ride‑hailing surges.  
- **Surge zones** – monitor the app’s heat map; position yourself near high‑demand areas before the surge hits.  
- **Multi‑app stacking** – run two or more apps simultaneously (e.g., Uber Eats + DoorDash) to fill gaps between rides.  
- **Mileage optimization** – plan routes that minimize deadhead miles; use navigation apps with real‑time traffic to avoid congestion.  
- **Vehicle efficiency** – keep tires inflated, perform regular maintenance, and consider a fuel‑efficient or electric vehicle to lower operating costs.  
- **Customer ratings** – maintain a 4.9+ rating to unlock higher‑pay incentives and priority dispatches.  

## Safety & Legal Checklist  
- **Insurance** – confirm you have both personal liability coverage and any platform‑required commercial coverage.  
- **Background checks** – keep your criminal and driving records clean; address any discrepancies promptly.  
- **Tax considerations** – treat earnings as self‑employment income; set aside ~25‑30 % for federal/state taxes, and track deductible expenses (fuel, maintenance, phone plan, car washes).  
- **Driver safety** – use a phone mount, keep the volume low, and follow local traffic laws; carry a first‑aid kit and emergency contacts.  
- **Legal compliance** – verify local regulations for ride‑hailing or delivery services (permits, vehicle inspections, zoning).
