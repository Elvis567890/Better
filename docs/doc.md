## Introduction
Welcome to the **Self‑Drive Boat Services Guide**. This document provides everything you need to know to rent, operate, and enjoy a self‑drive boat safely and responsibly. Whether you’re a first‑time renter or an experienced mariner, follow these guidelines to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience on the water.

## Getting Started

### Eligibility & Licensing
| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Age** | Minimum 18 years (21 for high‑performance vessels) |
| **Boating License** | Valid US Coast Guard (USCG) license, local boating permit, or a recognized equivalent. Some locations accept a **Boating Safety Course Certificate** (e.g., NAUI, US Power Squadrons). |
| **Identification** | Government‑issued photo ID (driver’s license, passport). |
| **Insurance** | Optional personal watercraft insurance recommended; basic coverage is included in the rental fee. |

### Booking Your Boat
1. **Select a Vessel** – Browse the fleet by size, power, and amenities.  
2. **Check Availability** – Real‑time calendar shows open slots.  
3. **Reserve** – Provide name, contact info, license details, and preferred dates.  
4. **Confirmation** – Receive an email with reservation number, boat specs, and a link to the pre‑rental checklist.  

> **Tip:** Book at least 48 hours in advance for peak season (June‑August) to guarantee availability.

### Payment & Cancellation Policies
- **Payment Methods:** Credit/Debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, or direct bank transfer.  
- **Deposit:** 20 % of total rental cost (refundable upon satisfactory return).  
- **Cancellation:** Free up to 24 hours before start time; 50 % charge within 24 hours; no refund after the rental period begins.

## Safety First

### Required Safety Equipment
| Item | Minimum Quantity | Placement |
|------|------------------|-----------|
| Personal Flotation Devices (PFDs) | 1 per passenger (USCG‑approved) | Easily accessible |
| Fire Extinguisher | 1 (rated for Class B) | Near engine compartment |
| Sound Signaling Device (whistle/horn) | 1 | Visible & functional |
| Visual Distress Signals (VDS) | 1 (flares or electronic) | Stowed securely |
| First‑Aid Kit | 1 (complete) | Dry, sealed container |
| Bilge Pump (manual/electric) | 1 | Engine area |
| Anchor & Rode | 1 set (appropriate weight) | Bow or stern as needed |

### Pre‑Departure Checklist
- [ ] Verify all safety equipment is present and in good condition.  
- [ ] Inspect hull for cracks, loose fittings, or damage.  
- [ ] Check fuel level and fuel caps for leaks.  
- [ ] Test engine start, throttle response, and steering.  
- [ ] Confirm navigation lights and battery charge.  
- [ ] Review weather forecast and tide tables.  

### Emergency Procedures
1. **Man Overboard** – Immediately sound the horn, turn the boat downwind, and deploy a life ring.  
2. **Engine Failure** – Deploy anchor, signal distress using VDS, and contact the nearest Coast Guard station (VHF channel 16).  
3. **Fire** – Use the fire extinguisher on the source, shut off fuel supply, and evacuate if necessary.

## Operating the Boat

### Basic Controls Overview
| Control | Function |
|---------|----------|
| **Throttle Lever** | Adjusts engine speed; forward (push) and reverse (pull). |
| **Steering Wheel / Tiller** | Changes heading; turn left/right. |
| **Gear Shift** (if applicable) | Selects forward, neutral, or reverse. |
| **Trim Tab** | Adjusts boat’s angle for optimal performance. |
| **Bilge Pump Switch** | Activates pump to remove water from hull. |
| **VHF Radio** | Communication; channel 16 for distress, channel 13 for bridge-to-bridge. |

### Navigational Rules (COLREGs)
- **Right‑of‑Way:** Power-driven vessels must give way to sailing vessels and vessels constrained by their draft.  
- **Crossing Situation:** The vessel with the other on its starboard side must keep out of the way.  
- **Overtaking:** Overtaking vessel must keep clear of the vessel being overtaken.  
- **Lights:** Display proper navigation lights from sunset to sunrise (red‑green‑white).  

### Speed Limits & No‑Go Zones
| Area | Speed Limit | Restrictions |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Marinas & Harbors** | ≤ 5 knots | No wake zones; watch for other traffic. |
| **Protected Wildlife Areas** | ≤ 3 knots | No anchoring; maintain distance from nesting sites. |
| **Designated No‑Wake Zones** | 0 knots (anchor only) | Typically near docks, piers, and shorelines. |
| **Open Water** | Up to vessel’s rated maximum (usually 25–35 knots) | Observe local ordinances. |

## Maintenance & Care

### Daily Cleaning & Inspection
- Rinse hull with fresh water after each use to remove salt and debris.  
- Wipe down deck, seats, and control panels.  
- Empty bilge and check for water accumulation.  

### Fuel Management
- Use only the fuel grade specified in the vessel’s manual (typically 87‑octane gasoline).  
- Fill the tank no more than 95 % full to allow for expansion.  
- Record fuel level at start and end of rental; report any leaks immediately.  

### Reporting Damage
- Document any damage with photos and a written description.  
- Notify the service provider within 2 hours of return.  
- Minor scratches may be charged a flat fee; structural damage will be assessed for repair costs.

## Environmental Responsibility
- **No Dumping:** Do not discharge waste, oil, or fuel into the water. Use designated pump‑out stations.  
- **Wildlife Protection:** Keep a safe distance from marine animals; avoid feeding or chasing them.  
- **Noise Pollution:** Respect quiet hours (typically 10 pm–7 am) in residential marinas.  
- **Leave No Trace:** Pack out all trash and personal belongings.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Do I need a captain’s license?** | A USCG‑issued boating license or a recognized safety course certificate is sufficient for most self‑drive rentals. |
| **Can I bring pets?** | Yes, but pets must be leashed or contained, and owners are responsible for cleaning up after them. |
| **What if the weather turns bad?** | Return to the dock immediately if conditions become unsafe. The rental agreement includes a “weather‑related cancellation” clause with a partial refund. |
| **Is there a minimum rental time?** | Minimum is 2 hours; half‑day (4 h) and full‑day (8 h) packages are also offered. |
| **Are there any hidden fees?** | All fees (fuel surcharge, cleaning, insurance) are disclosed before booking. Late returns incur a $25 per‑hour charge. |
| **Can I bring my own fishing gear?** | Yes, but ensure gear does not damage the boat or other users. Some vessels have dedicated rod holders. |
| **What if I run out of fuel?** | Contact the support line; a fuel delivery service can meet you at a pre‑agreed location for an additional fee. |
