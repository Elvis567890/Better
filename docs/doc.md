### 4. Approach & Methodology  

1. **Rapid Assessment** – Leverage existing data and quick stakeholder interviews to surface low‑hanging fruit.  
2. **Impact‑Effort Matrix** – Plot opportunities to focus on those delivering the greatest ROI with minimal resources.  
3. **Lean Pilot** – Build a minimum viable solution, test in a controlled environment, and iterate based on real‑time feedback.  
4. **Capability Building** – Conduct hands‑on workshops to embed the quick‑win methodology within the client team.  
5. **Metrics‑Driven Review** – Use pre‑defined KPIs to quantify success and create a repeatable framework for future quick‑wins.
