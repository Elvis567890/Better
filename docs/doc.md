### 📌 What Is Self‑Drive?

Self‑Drive is a turnkey platform that lets you turn any vehicle into a revenue‑generating asset. Whether you own a car, van, or electric scooter, our technology and network enable you to:

- **Rent it out instantly** on trusted marketplaces.  
- **Earn passive income** while you sleep.  
- **Maintain full control** over availability, pricing, and usage.
