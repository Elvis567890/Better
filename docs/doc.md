## Self‑Drive Service Agreement

**This Self‑Drive Service Agreement** (“Agreement”) is entered into as of **[Effective Date]** by and between:

- **Provider:** *[Company Name]*, a corporation organized under the laws of *[State/Country]*, with its principal place of business at *[Address]* (hereinafter “Provider”), and  
- **Customer:** *[Customer Name]*, residing at *[Customer Address]* (hereinafter “Customer”).

Collectively referred to as the “Parties”.

---

### 1. Scope of Service
1.1 **Service Description** – Provider shall make available to Customer a self‑drive vehicle (the “Vehicle”) for personal or business use in accordance with the terms set forth herein.  
1.2 **Vehicle Details** – The Vehicle shall be a *[Make, Model, Year]*, identified by VIN *[VIN]*, and shall be delivered in clean, operable condition with a full fuel tank.  
1.3 **Authorized Use** – Customer may use the Vehicle only for lawful purposes, within the geographic limits of *[Country/Region]*, and must comply with all applicable traffic laws and regulations.  
1.4 **Prohibited Use** – The Vehicle shall not be used for:  
   - Commercial transport of passengers or goods for hire.  
   - Racing, off‑road driving, or any activity that exceeds the Vehicle’s specifications.  
   - Transfer to any third party without Provider’s prior written consent.

---

### 2. Payment Terms
2.1 **Rental Fee** – Customer shall pay a rental fee of **$[Amount] per day** (or as otherwise specified in the attached rate schedule).  
2.2 **Security Deposit** – A refundable security deposit of **$[Amount]** is required prior to vehicle delivery. The deposit will be returned within **[X] business days** after the Vehicle is returned in satisfactory condition.  
2.3 **Payment Method** – All payments shall be made by credit card, debit card, or bank transfer to the account designated by Provider.  
2.4 **Late Payment** – Late payments shall incur interest at **[X]% per month** (or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower).  
2.5 **Taxes & Fees** – All applicable taxes, surcharges, and fees are the responsibility of the Customer and shall be included in the final invoice.

---

### 3. Liability
3.1 **Damage to Vehicle** – Customer is liable for any loss, theft, or damage to the Vehicle, including but not limited to collision, vandalism, fire, or natural disasters, unless caused by Provider’s negligence.  
3.2 **Third‑Party Claims** – Customer shall indemnify and hold Provider harmless from any third‑party claims, damages, or injuries arising from Customer’s use of the Vehicle.  
3.3 **Limitation of Liability** – Provider’s total liability for any claim arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount of fees paid by Customer under this Agreement, except for liability arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct.  
3.4 **Force Majeure** – Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations due to causes beyond its reasonable control (e.g., natural disasters, war, governmental actions).

---

### 4. Cancellation Policy
4.1 **Customer Cancellation** – Customer may cancel the reservation up to **[X] hours** before the scheduled pickup time and receive a full refund of any prepaid fees, less the security deposit. Cancellations made later will forfeit **[Y]%** of the rental fee.  
4.2 **Provider Cancellation** – Provider reserves the right to cancel the reservation at any time due to vehicle unavailability, maintenance, or other legitimate reasons. In such event, Provider shall refund all prepaid amounts and may offer a comparable replacement vehicle, subject to Customer’s acceptance.  
4.3 **No‑Show** – Failure to pick up the Vehicle at the agreed time without prior notice shall be deemed a “no‑show,” and Customer shall be charged the full rental fee for the first day plus any applicable fees.

---

### 5. Insurance Requirements
5.1 **Mandatory Coverage** – Customer must maintain the following insurance coverage for the duration of the rental:  
   - **Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)** – Minimum coverage of **$[Amount]**.  
   - **Liability Insurance** – Minimum bodily injury and property damage liability of **$[Amount]** per incident.  
   - **Personal Accident Insurance** – Optional, but recommended.  
5.2 **Proof of Insurance** – Customer shall provide a valid insurance certificate or proof of coverage prior to vehicle pickup.  
5.3 **Deductibles** – In the event of a claim, Customer shall be responsible for any deductible amounts specified in the insurance policy.  
5.4 **Additional Insurance** – Provider may offer supplemental insurance products (e.g., personal effects coverage) at an additional cost, which Customer may elect to purchase.

---

### 6. Safety Guidelines
6.1 **Vehicle Inspection** – Customer shall conduct a pre‑rental inspection with Provider staff, noting any existing damage on the provided inspection checklist.  
6.2 **Operating the Vehicle** – Customer must possess a valid driver’s license appropriate for the Vehicle class and must not operate the Vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any impairing substances.  
6.3 **Maintenance & Repairs** – Customer shall not perform any repairs or modifications to the Vehicle. In case of a breakdown, Customer must contact Provider’s roadside assistance line **[Phone Number]** immediately.  
6.4 **Emergency Procedures** – In the event of an accident or emergency, Customer shall:  
   - Ensure safety of all occupants.  
   - Contact emergency services (dial 911 or local equivalent).  
   - Notify Provider within **[X] hours** and provide a detailed incident report.  
6.5 **Return Condition** – Upon return, the Vehicle must be in the same condition as received, normal wear and tear excepted. Fuel level must be at least **[X]%** of the level at pickup.

- **Severability:** If any provision is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.  
- **Notices:** All notices shall be delivered in writing to the addresses set forth above or to such other address as a Party may designate in writing.
